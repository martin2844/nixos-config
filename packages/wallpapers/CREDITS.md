# Wallpaper sources and credits

12 curated images. Original colours are preserved. GNOME JPEG XL images are
resized to at most 3840×3840 and exported as JPEG for fast gallery loading.
Space JPEGs and Plasma PNGs are copied unchanged. Hyprpaper crops to fill the
screen; this is SDR imagery selected for contrast, not HDR mastering.
